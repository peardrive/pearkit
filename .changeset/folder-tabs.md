Add `FolderTabs` and `FolderTabPage`: tabs whose open tab is lit and opens straight into its page, with the page's `primary` edge running on from the tab, across the top, round the corners and fading out down the sides over a barely-there frame. Drawn with react-native-svg, so it is the same on web and native. `FolderTabPage` frames any panel on its own.
