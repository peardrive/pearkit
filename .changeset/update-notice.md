New **`UpdateNotice`**: the row at the bottom of the sidebar, above Settings, when a newer version is waiting on the app's update channel. One press downloads, installs and restarts (no trip into Settings). States `available` (with "N behind"), `downloading` (percent and a thin bar), `restarting` and `error` (press to try again); an icon with a dot and a tooltip in a collapsed sidebar. PearDrive's look, so PearDrive can switch to it.
