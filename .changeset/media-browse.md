Add the media browse pair: `Masonry` (a wall of mixed-height tiles in balanced columns — `columns` fixed or computed from `minColumnWidth`, heights balanced by `getAspectRatio`, with the pure `masonryColumns()` geometry for virtualized or canvas hosts) and `MediaTile` (one tile of that wall: picture or kind icon, corner badge, title and meta on a scrim, selection ring, favorite star, and a host-owned `preview` slot driven by `onHoverChange` — the tile never owns playback). All React Native primitives — the same components run on Electron and on the phone.
