New **`UpdatesPanel`** (and `ReleaseDialog`): the Updates block of an app's settings, laid out as PearDrive's. **Latest** carries the answer (up to date, or "Update to x" with an "n behind" tag) with Check beside it; **Versions** is a framed list, the running one marked "Your version", the newest with the bright Install, any other installable too; the channel and its publisher sit behind **Advanced**; pressing a version opens its notes, date, size and signature in a window. The app's update channel drives it. `UpdateNotice` now says "Downloading update…", as PearDrive does.
