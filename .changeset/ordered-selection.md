Choosing things in order: `OrderTile`, `OrderBadge` and `useOrderedSelection`. A tap gives a tile the next number (1, 2, 3) in place of a check mark, a tap on a chosen tile lets it go and the ones after it move up, and number 1 leads. The rule on its own (`toggleOrder`, `orderNumber`, `sendLast`, `sendFirst`, `inOrder`) is exported for pages that are not React.
