New `SocialBar` (like, comment, share with their numbers, also as a vertical rail over a picture), `CommentThread` / `CommentRow` (comments with the answers under them, a heart and Reply on each) and `CommentBox` (a box that grows with its text; Enter sends on a keyboard).
