`AskCard`: an agent asks a person for a permission, in the conversation where it works. Who asks, the tool and the exact thing it would do (the whole input a tap away), three answers for the people who may give them (Allow once, Always, Deny), a lock line for everyone else, and the outcome once answered ("allowed once by guy (macbook)"). The rules (`askStatus`, `choiceWords`, `askTimeLeft`, `askOutcomeLine`) live in `askLogic`, tested.
