Add the capture pieces a recording app's dock is made of: `RecordButton` (arm, record, recording, busy: one round key, a pulsing square while recording, a label or clock beside it), `DeviceToggle` (a camera or microphone as a capsule: the body switches it on and off, the caret opens its options, off is tinted and slashed, a level along the bottom; `compact` for a thumb) and `LevelMeter` (one thin bar, red when it clips). New colour token `rec`, the record red, which is not `danger`. New icons `screen` and `settings` on the 24px / 1.5px grid. `IconButton` gains `size="xl"` (50px, a dock's button).
